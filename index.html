Semiconductor Packaging
Amkor Technology
Broke ground Oct 2025 on 104-acre campus in Peoria Innovation Core. Two-phase project: 750,000+ sq ft cleanroom; first facility complete mid-2027, production early 2028. Customers: Apple and NVIDIA. Supported by CHIPS Act ($900M federal) and state/local incentives. 3,000 jobs at full build-out.

Healthcare / Biomedical
Mayo Clinic Arizona + ASU Health
Mayo Clinic: nearly $1.9B expansion of Phoenix campus (56th St & Mayo Blvd), adds 1.2M sq ft, 11 operating rooms, 48 beds. Completion targeted 2031. 3,500 additional jobs. City separately investing $50M in new ASU Health building on Phoenix Biomedical Core — merging medical and engineering education.
$1.9B Mayo · $50M City/ASU
Clean Energy / Battery
LG Energy Solution
$5.5B battery complex in Queen Creek (East Valley). Cylindrical EV battery plant (46-Series) construction complete late 2025, production expected 2026. ESS LFP plant paused pending market conditions. ~2,800 jobs Phase 1. First-ever U.S. stand-alone cylindrical battery plant.
$5.5B · Queen Creek
Advanced Mfg / AI

Opened Factory 3 (F3) on Jan 29, 2026 — 290,000 sq ft AI-powered manufacturing and software hub at The Cubes Mesa Gateway. Already in production: precision components and mission-critical systems for aerospace and defense. 350+ high-wage jobs.
